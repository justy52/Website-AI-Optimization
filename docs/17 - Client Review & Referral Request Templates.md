CLIENT REVIEW & REFERRAL REQUEST TEMPLATES  
Website and AI Optimization For Small Businesses

AI-FIRST OPERATING PRINCIPLE -> AUTHORITATIVE UPDATE

The business is intentionally designed around AI-agent-assisted fulfillment. AI agents perform the majority of repeatable collection, monitoring, analysis, drafting, prioritization, documentation, reporting, and safe internal execution. Human time is reserved for strategy, client relationships, judgment, quality control, and approval of consequential actions.

Agent permission levels:
1. OBSERVE -> read, crawl, monitor, measure, compare, and report. May run automatically.
2. PREPARE -> draft content, recommendations, schema, metadata, code changes, reports, tasks, and client communications. Output remains a draft until the applicable approval policy is satisfied.
3. EXECUTE -> perform only explicitly allowlisted, low-risk, reversible actions. Live-site publishing, destructive changes, contractual commitments, pricing changes, client-facing sends, and other consequential actions require human approval until a later policy explicitly permits otherwise.

Every material agent action must be tenant-scoped, logged, attributable to an agent/run, evidence-backed where factual claims are made, bounded by cost/time limits, and recoverable or safely retryable.


AI TRANSPARENCY RULE

Never imply every line of work was manually produced if automation materially assisted it. At the same time, do not make AI the centerpiece of review/referral requests. The client should evaluate the quality, clarity, responsiveness, and business results of the service.


PURPOSE  
Use these templates after a successful project or meaningful milestone. The goal is to make it easy for satisfied clients to leave a review, provide a testimonial, or introduce another small-business owner who may need help.

OPERATING RULES  
- Ask only after the client has received real value.  
- Personalize the message with a specific result or completed project.  
- Make the request easy to complete.  
- Do not pressure clients or repeatedly chase them.  
- Never offer compensation for a positive review.  
- If offering any referral incentive in the future, keep it separate from review requests and clearly disclose applicable terms.

WHEN TO ASK  
Good moments include:  
- After project completion and client approval  
- After a measurable improvement the client is pleased with  
- After the client gives unsolicited positive feedback  
- After resolving a meaningful website problem  
- After a successful first month or quarter of monthly optimization

Avoid asking when:  
- There is an unresolved complaint  
- A major deliverable is overdue  
- The client is waiting on a refund, credit, or correction  
- The client has expressed dissatisfaction

13. REVIEW / REFERRAL TRACKING  
Track internally:  
- Client name  
- Project completion date  
- Review requested date  
- Review received: Yes / No  
- Testimonial permission: Yes / No / Not requested  
- Referral requested date  
- Referral received: Yes / No  
- Referral name/business  
- Result of referral

14. INTERNAL QUALITY RULE  
Do not treat every completed project as automatically worthy of a review request. First ask:  
- Did we deliver the agreed scope?  
- Is the client satisfied?  
- Were problems handled professionally?  
- Would asking now feel natural?

If the answer is no, fix the client experience before asking for public praise.
