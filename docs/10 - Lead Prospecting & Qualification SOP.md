LEAD PROSPECTING & QUALIFICATION SOP  
Website and AI Optimization For Small Businesses

AI-FIRST OPERATING PRINCIPLE -> AUTHORITATIVE UPDATE

The business is intentionally designed around AI-agent-assisted fulfillment. AI agents perform the majority of repeatable collection, monitoring, analysis, drafting, prioritization, documentation, reporting, and safe internal execution. Human time is reserved for strategy, client relationships, judgment, quality control, and approval of consequential actions.

Agent permission levels:
1. OBSERVE -> read, crawl, monitor, measure, compare, and report. May run automatically.
2. PREPARE -> draft content, recommendations, schema, metadata, code changes, reports, tasks, and client communications. Output remains a draft until the applicable approval policy is satisfied.
3. EXECUTE -> perform only explicitly allowlisted, low-risk, reversible actions. Live-site publishing, destructive changes, contractual commitments, pricing changes, client-facing sends, and other consequential actions require human approval until a later policy explicitly permits otherwise.

Every material agent action must be tenant-scoped, logged, attributable to an agent/run, evidence-backed where factual claims are made, bounded by cost/time limits, and recoverable or safely retryable.


UPDATED QUALIFICATION ECONOMICS

A good-fit prospect can reasonably benefit from a $495 Digital Visibility Audit, a $1,500+ Optimization Launch, and potentially a $750-$2,000/month optimization plan. Prospecting agents may identify and rank leads using public business/website information, but cold outreach must remain compliant, non-deceptive, and subject to human-approved messaging rules.


PURPOSE  
This SOP defines how to identify, qualify, contact, and advance prospective small-business clients without wasting time on poor-fit leads or giving away the full value of the paid audit during sales outreach.

PRIMARY GOAL  
Find small businesses with clear website or AI-optimization opportunities, make a concise first contact, qualify the business, and move good-fit prospects into the paid Digital Visibility Audit.

IDEAL PROSPECT PROFILE  
Prioritize businesses that:  
- Are actively operating and generating revenue  
- Depend on local or online leads  
- Already have a website  
- Have visible website problems or missed opportunities  
- Appear to have a reachable owner or decision-maker  
- Sell services or products with enough value that better website performance matters  
- Are not obviously locked into a complex enterprise marketing stack  
- Can reasonably benefit from a $495 audit and $1,500+ Optimization Launch

GOOD EARLY TARGET INDUSTRIES  
Focus first on service businesses where calls, forms, appointments, or quote requests matter, including:  
- Contractors and home services  
- Landscaping  
- HVAC  
- Plumbing  
- Electrical  
- Roofing  
- Fencing  
- Cleaning  
- Auto services  
- Professional services  
- Local medical/dental practices where appropriate  
- Fitness/wellness businesses  
- Small retailers with meaningful local traffic  
- Specialty trades

LEAD SOURCES  
Use a mix of:  
- Google Maps / Google Business Profile discovery  
- Local search results  
- Chamber of commerce directories  
- Local Facebook/business groups  
- Referral partners  
- Existing business relationships  
- LinkedIn  
- Community networking  
- Local directories  
- Website footers that identify current web providers  
- Businesses encountered in daily life

DO NOT MASS-SPAM  
The preferred approach is targeted outreach based on something real found on the prospect's website. Each message should contain a specific observation so it does not look like generic agency spam.

PRE-OUTREACH QUICK REVIEW  
Spend only enough time to identify 2-4 meaningful observations.

Check:  
- Does the site work well on mobile?  
- Is the primary call to action obvious?  
- Is contact information easy to find?  
- Are service areas/services clear?  
- Are there broken pages or links?  
- Does the site load slowly?  
- Is the design outdated enough to reduce trust?  
- Are titles/headings obviously weak or missing?  
- Does the content clearly explain what the business does?  
- Are location and service details clear enough for local search and AI discovery?  
- Are trust signals visible?  
- Is there an obvious analytics, form, or conversion issue?

Do not perform the full audit at this stage.

LEAD RECORD  
Every prospect should have a simple record containing:  
- Business name  
- Website URL  
- Contact name if known  
- Email  
- Phone  
- City/service area  
- Industry  
- Lead source  
- 2-4 observations  
- Estimated fit: High / Medium / Low  
- Current status  
- Last contact date  
- Next action  
- Notes

QUALIFICATION SCORE  
Use the following simple framework.

HIGH FIT  
- Website clearly needs improvement  
- Business appears active  
- Website matters to lead generation  
- Decision-maker can likely be reached  
- Scope appears manageable  
- Business likely has budget for paid optimization

MEDIUM FIT  
- Some opportunity is visible  
- Business appears active  
- Need or budget is unclear  
- Decision-maker is not yet known

LOW FIT  
- Business appears inactive  
- No real website need  
- Very small hobby operation with little commercial value  
- Major custom/e-commerce complexity outside current capability  
- Prospect is clearly seeking free consulting only  
- Strong signs of payment or communication risk

FIRST-CONTACT OBJECTIVE  
The goal of first contact is not to sell the entire project. The goal is to create enough interest for a brief conversation or move directly to the paid audit.

FIRST-CONTACT STRUCTURE  
1. Personal opening  
2. One specific website observation  
3. Why it may matter to the business  
4. Mention that you specialize in website and AI optimization for small businesses  
5. Offer a low-friction next step

EXAMPLE POSITIONING  
"I noticed a couple of things on your site that may be making it harder for customers to contact you and for search/AI systems to clearly understand your services. I help small businesses identify and fix those kinds of issues. I offer a focused Digital Visibility Audit that shows what matters most and what I would fix first."

Do not overstate results or imply that an issue is definitely costing a specific amount of revenue unless the data supports that claim.

DISCOVERY / QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS  
Ask enough to understand the opportunity:  
- What is the main job you want the website to do?  
- What types of leads or customers are most valuable?  
- Are you happy with the number/quality of website leads now?  
- Who currently manages the website?  
- What platform is it built on?  
- Have you had recent website or SEO work done?  
- Do you use Google Analytics/Search Console?  
- What is the biggest frustration with the website?  
- Are there services or locations you want to grow?  
- Are you interested in improving visibility in AI-assisted search as well as traditional search?  
- Who makes the final decision on website work?

PAID AUDIT TRANSITION  
When the prospect is qualified, explain the audit clearly:

The Digital Visibility Audit is a paid diagnostic engagement. Standard price: $495.

It gives the client:  
- A structured review of the current site  
- Prioritized issues  
- Website performance/usability findings  
- Search and local visibility findings  
- AI discoverability/readiness findings  
- Conversion opportunities  
- Recommended next steps

The audit is designed to avoid vague proposals and give both sides a clear scope before optimization work begins.

WHEN TO WALK AWAY  
Do not chase prospects who:  
- Repeatedly demand extensive free analysis  
- Refuse to identify a decision-maker  
- Cannot provide basic project information  
- Show clear disrespect or unrealistic expectations  
- Expect guaranteed rankings or guaranteed revenue  
- Want unlimited work for a very low budget  
- Refuse reasonable payment terms  
- Request unethical or deceptive tactics

FOLLOW-UP CADENCE  
Use a light follow-up approach. Example:  
- Initial contact  
- Follow-up 1 after several business days  
- Follow-up 2 roughly one week later  
- Final close-the-loop message

Do not repeatedly message unresponsive prospects indefinitely.

PIPELINE STAGES  
New Lead -> Reviewed -> Contacted -> Responded -> Qualified -> Audit Offered -> Audit Sold -> Proposal Opportunity -> Not Now / Lost

WEEKLY PROSPECTING ROUTINE  
At least once per week:  
- Add new prospects  
- Review outstanding follow-ups  
- Remove poor-fit leads  
- Advance qualified prospects  
- Track audit conversion rate  
- Record objections heard repeatedly  
- Note which industries respond best

KEY METRICS  
Track:  
- New leads added  
- Contacts attempted  
- Responses  
- Qualified prospects  
- Audits sold  
- Audit conversion rate  
- Optimization proposals generated  
- Optimization Launchs sold  
- Revenue by lead source

RULES  
- Be specific, not spammy.  
- Sell the audit before giving away the audit.  
- Qualify for fit, not just interest.  
- Centralize notes and next actions.  
- Never promise rankings, AI placement, or revenue.  
- Protect time by walking away from clearly poor-fit leads.
