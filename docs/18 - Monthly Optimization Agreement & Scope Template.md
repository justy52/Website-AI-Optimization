For smaller local businesses that need continuous professional monitoring and incremental improvement.

- Up to 2 hours of website optimization/updates as defined by the public package.
- One existing-page optimization per month.

Internal human fulfillment target: 45-60 minutes/month after automation matures.

### Growth -> $1,250/month
Expected primary plan for established local businesses.
- Everything in Essentials.
- Deeper keyword, competitor, local SEO, citation, internal-link, conversion, and service/location strategy.
- Up to 4 hours of website optimization/updates as defined by the public package.
- One major content deliverable and one existing-page optimization per month.

Internal human fulfillment target: approximately 1.5 hours/month.

### Pro -> $2,000/month
For more aggressive growth, competitive markets, or broader expansion.
- Everything in Growth.
- Up to 7 hours of website optimization/development work as defined by the public package.
- Two major content assets and two existing-page optimizations per month.
- Expanded keyword/competitor/content-gap/backlink opportunity work.
- Expanded AI visibility, structured data, conversion testing, and quarterly strategy reviews.

Internal human fulfillment target: approximately 3 hours/month.


### How the Website-Work Allowance Works

The included **2 / 4 / 7 hours of website optimization and update work** in Essentials, Growth, and Pro is a client-facing allowance for manual or custom implementation work that requires direct hands-on editing, development, configuration, or review.

Routine AI-agent work does **not** consume this allowance. Automated monitoring, data collection, analysis, opportunity detection, first drafts, recurring checks, report generation, and other routine agent operations are part of the service itself.

The allowance is not convertible to cash, does not roll over unless the agreement explicitly says otherwise, and is not an unlimited-development commitment. Work that materially exceeds the package scope is quoted separately.


TERM AND BILLING

- After the initial term, month-to-month with 30 days' notice.
- Third-party costs are separate unless explicitly included.
- No unlimited development.
- No guarantee of rankings, AI placement, lead volume, or revenue.
- Material live-site publishing or other consequential actions remain subject to the agreed approval policy.

PURPOSE  
This template defines the standard ongoing Monthly Website, Search & AI Optimization service after an initial Optimization Launch. The goal is to give the client predictable monitoring and continuous improvement without creating an unlimited-development obligation.

1. SERVICE OVERVIEW  
The monthly optimization plan is designed to maintain website health, identify new opportunities, and complete a limited amount of ongoing optimization work each month.

Typical areas of focus may include:  
- Website health and functionality  
- Performance monitoring  
- Broken links or minor issues  
- Search visibility and technical SEO checks  
- Local SEO alignment  
- AI discoverability and content structure  
- Analytics and conversion review  
- Minor content, metadata, or call-to-action improvements  
- Quarterly priority planning  
- Client reporting

2. INCLUDED MONTHLY SERVICES  
Website Health

- Review basic technical SEO health  
- Make small title, description, heading, internal-linking, or content-structure improvements when appropriate  
- Review local business information consistency when relevant

- Review whether important business/service information remains clear and current
